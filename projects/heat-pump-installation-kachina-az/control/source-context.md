## Market facts
- Kachina Village, Coconino County, AZ (ZIP 86005), unincorporated CDP ~8 mi
  south of Flagstaff on I-17. Elevation 6,798 ft; Köppen Dsb; IECC zone 5B
  behaving near zone 6; ~7,000 HDD; winter 99% design temp low single digits
  °F; 80–100 in. annual snowfall; no month averages a daily high above ~72°F.
- Utilities: APS (electric); UniSource Energy Services (natural gas — serves
  Kachina Village but not every street/lot); KVID (water/sewer); Highlands
  Fire District; FUSD schools.
- ~2,500–3,200 residents; ~75% owner-occupied; median home value ≈ $406K
  (verify at review); ~50% of adults bachelor's+; bedroom community for
  Flagstaff/NAU.
- Wildland-urban interface in Coconino NF ponderosa; prescribed burns via
  Mormon Lake Ranger District; smoke events are normal.
- Unincorporated: mechanical permits and inspections through **Coconino County
  Community Development — never the City of Flagstaff.**

## Brand identity
- Promise: cold-climate heat pumps engineered for 6,800 ft and a Kachina
  Village winter — sized with a real load calculation, commissioned on paper,
  backed by a licensed Arizona contractor.
- Differentiators: (1) altitude-aware design (air ~20% thinner: airflow,
  static pressure, blower/duct sizing, venting); (2) cold-climate equipment
  only, quoted from NEEP ccASHP list with published 5°F capacity/COP;
  (3) corridor-local (Kachina Village, Mountainaire, Munds Park, Forest
  Highlands, Flagstaff), not a Valley company; (4) documentation: Manual J,
  Manual S, AHRI certificate, commissioning sheet — customer keeps every one.
- Proprietary process names (ours; never dressed as third-party credentials):
  **The 6,800-Foot Load Check**, **The Cold-Night Guarantee Design Standard**,
  **The Snow-Line Siting Standard**, **The Handover Packet**.
- We do NOT do: wood/pellet stoves or fireplaces; propane tank sets, gas runs
  beyond appliance connection, LP delivery; standalone solar PV/battery/
  rewiring (panel work via licensed electrical partner); commercial
  refrigeration/RTUs; standalone duct cleaning; 24/7 emergency dispatch as a
  headline promise.

## Offers
1. Ducted cold-climate heat pump installation (furnace/air-handler changeout,
   duct repair included) — top revenue with duct correction + filter bundled.
2. Ductless / mini-split and multi-zone — no-duct homes, additions, cabins.
3. Dual-fuel with UES gas furnace, deliberate switchover point — strategic
   growth: fast yes, makes us incumbent for the eventual full replacement.
4. Propane→heat-pump and electric-resistance→heat-pump conversions — the two
   highest-savings jobs in this market.
5. Replacement of failed/end-of-life systems. Best conversion rates: failed
   system, propane conversion after a winter bill, single-zone ductless.
6. Attached services: panel/circuit coordination, snow stands/elevated pads,
   line sets, smart thermostat, MERV-13 filtration upgrade.
- Ladder: premium = multi-zone variable-capacity/full duct replacement; mid =
  ducted changeout, dual-fuel; entry = single-zone ductless, cooling-add;
  recurring = maintenance agreement (retention tool); **loss leader = free
  in-home load calculation + written proposal — the most prominent CTA.**
  Never a loss leader: diagnostic-only visits.
- Seasonality: installs April–October ("book before the snow"); peak intent
  Aug–Oct after first cold night; Jan–Mar = propane-bill quote season, installs
  scheduled for spring; only manufacturer/distributor promotions may be
  "limited time," always dated.

## Regulatory & licensing facts
- Arizona ROC license required for contracting ≥$1,000 and any permitted work;
  relevant classes R-39R / CR-39 / C-39 / CR-79; unlicensed contracting is a
  Class 1 misdemeanor (A.R.S. §32-1164).
- A.R.S. §32-1124(B): ROC number on advertising; for websites, licensee name +
  ROC number prominently on the home page. **Practical rule: partner's ROC
  number in every footer and on the home page.**
- Coconino County issues permits/inspections for Kachina Village.
- Arizona Residential Contractors' Recovery Fund: legitimate trust point.
- EPA Section 608; A2L handling rules for R-454B/R-32.
- HOA/design review in Forest Highlands and parts of Munds Park (placement,
  screening, sound). Utility locates for trenching near easements.
