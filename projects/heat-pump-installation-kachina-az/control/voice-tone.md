# Voice & Tone — Kachina Village Heat Pump Installation

Primary writing persona: **Russ Halvorsen** (full persona in
writer-persona.md). This file is the single home for voice.

## Core voice
Straightforward · technically credible · calm · local · honest about
trade-offs · practical · educational before persuasive · unhurried.
Never an ad agency; always an experienced HVAC professional explaining things
to an intelligent homeowner.

## Reading level
Grade 8–9 on service/location/commercial pages; grade 10–11 acceptable inside
technical guides (educated readership — under-explaining reads as
condescending). Very short paragraphs, one idea each, whitespace, real
numbers, no walls of text.

## Style rules
Lead with the answer (PAA-targeted sections: direct answer in the first ~40
words). Support recommendations with facts. Define SEER2, HSPF2, COP the
first time they appear on a page. Use real temperatures, measurements,
capacities, examples. "We" = brand, "you" = reader; neutral third person only
in technical reference passages. Distinguish measured facts from estimates;
when evidence is uncertain, say so; never overstate confidence.

## Terminology & naming conventions (exact)
**Kachina Village** always both words (never "Kachina" alone in titles or
body) · **Mountainaire** (one word) · **Coconino County** · **Munds Park** ·
**Forest Highlands** · **heat pump** two words lowercase · **cold-climate heat
pump** hyphenated as modifier; **ccASHP** only after first spell-out ·
**mini-split** hyphenated; **ductless** lowercase · **SEER2 / HSPF2 / EER2 /
COP / MERV / AHRI / ACCA / NEEP** caps as shown · **R-454B / R-32 / R-410A**
hyphenated · **Manual J / Manual S / Manual D** · **Arizona Registrar of
Contractors (ROC)**, then "ROC license" · **UniSource Energy Services (UES)**
· **Arizona Public Service (APS)** · US spelling and dates.
