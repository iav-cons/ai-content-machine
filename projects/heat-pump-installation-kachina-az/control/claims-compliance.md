# Claims & Compliance — Kachina Village Heat Pump Installation

THE single canonical home for claim rails (source context holds the facts
behind them). SEO value never overrides factual truth or compliance. Accuracy
takes priority over conversion; publishing a limitation builds more trust than
hiding it.

## Rules requiring judgment (qualitative QC + human)
- Never present the brand as holding its own contractor license, crew,
  warehouse, or history. License language, exactly: "Installation work is
  performed by our licensed Arizona contracting partner, ROC #[number]."
  Never "our license." ROC number in every footer + home page (A.R.S.
  §32-1124).
- Never state or imply a dollar amount a visitor "will get" from Efficiency
  Arizona / HEAR; income-qualified, funding-limited, existing-heat-pump homes
  excluded; describe + link to the official page only.
- Savings/payback/operating-cost: always "estimated"/"modeled," dependent on
  home, usage, fuel costs, equipment; never a universal percentage; name the
  comparison basis.
- "Free estimate/assessment": say exactly what is free; no attached
  conditions left unstated.
- No invented reviews, ratings, awards, technician counts, install counts, or
  stock photos captioned as our team/trucks. FTC endorsement rules for any
  testimonial; disclose incentives.
- No certifications (NATE, BPI, ENERGY STAR Partner, dealer tiers) unless the
  partner holds the certificate — see Operations truth in source-context.
- No medical/health claims for air quality; filtration is comfort + particle
  capture, never disease prevention.
- Tax content: program status + link to IRS/official page; never filing
  advice. Incentive figures verified against the official source on the day
  of publication, every time.
- CO safety content: direct readers to leave and call 911, never to book.
- No fear/urgency manufacturing; no countdown timers; no fake scarcity; extra
  care with elderly-facing copy (real segment, real sensitivity).
- Never send an unincorporated-address reader to the City of Flagstaff permit
  portal — Coconino County only.
- Utilities (APS, UES), manufacturers, ENERGY STAR/AHRI/NEEP/ACCA, Coconino
  County: name factually; never imply endorsement, partnership, or approval.

## Always verify before publication
Partner ROC number + classification · county permit facts · manufacturer
capacity data (published 5°F figures for the exact model) · current incentive
status (dated) · utility service facts · warranty terms quoted exactly.

## Required trust signals
ROC number (verifiable on roc.az.gov) · county permit + passed inspection ·
written Manual J before equipment is named · Manual S selection · AHRI
matched-system certificate · commissioning sheet · Recovery Fund explanation.
