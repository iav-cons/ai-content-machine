# Audience / ICP — Kachina Village Heat Pump Installation

The single home for WHO. (Writer persona = writer-persona.md; how it sounds =
voice-tone.md.)

## Primary audience
Owner-occupiers in Kachina Village, Mountainaire, Forest Highlands, Munds
Park, south Flagstaff. Homes 1975–2005, 1,200–2,600 sq ft, heated by propane,
electric baseboard/wall units, aging gas furnaces, or wood stove + electric.
Age 38–72, joint decisions; HHI $70K–$180K; $8K–$25K available or financing.
NAU/FUSD staff, remote workers, retirees, trades, landlords/STR owners.
Secondary: Flagstaff-area landlord/STR owner with a Kachina/Munds Park
property wanting a low-callback system.

## Psychology
~50% bachelor's+; research hard, read spec sheets, resent condescension.
Motivated by fuel cost, cold rooms, staying put. Fears: heat pumps quitting in
cold, a winter electric bill replacing the propane bill, late panel-upgrade
surprises, out-of-town crews, units freezing in the yard. Decide over 2–8
weeks; failed-system and red-tag buyers decide in days and want a date, not a
price. Trust: license, permits, paperwork.

## Behaviors
Google/Local Pack, Nextdoor, neighbor referrals; contact 2–4 companies; won't
DIY but inspect the work; check ROC number, reviews naming their town, real
local photos; raise financing late.

## Top pre-purchase questions
1. Does a heat pump actually work at 5°F in Kachina Village? (the biggest)
2. All-in installed cost here? 3. Will it beat my propane bill — by how much?
4. Do I keep my furnace as backup? 5. What size for 1,800 sq ft at 6,800 ft?
6. Ductless or ducted for my house? 7. Any credits or rebates left? (no
federal 25C in 2026; possibly Efficiency Arizona if income-qualified)
8. What's the A2L/R-454B change? 9. Permit — who pulls it? 10. Will my panel
handle it? 11. How loud, and where does it go with the snow? 12. Install
duration — will I be without heat?

## Objections
"Heat pumps don't work in the cold" (1990s legacy belief — the primary
barrier) · sticker price vs like-for-like furnace · winter electric bill fear
· late panel-upgrade cost · out-of-town contractor distrust · snow/ice/defrost
worry · lost federal credit changing the math vs a 2025 quote never acted on.

## Decision triggers
First hard freeze (late Sep/Oct) · propane delivery or high winter bill ·
furnace failure / cracked exchanger / red-tag · home purchase + inspection
report · smoke events (filtration) · remodels/additions/ADUs · equipment past
15 years and rising R-410A service cost.

## Content preferences
Capacity-at-5°F data, altitude/sizing explainers, real installed cost ranges,
propane-vs-heat-pump math, ducted vs ductless, snow siting, permits, local
before/after cases, honest 2026 rebate status. Say: "sized with a real load
calculation, at 6,800 feet, installed by a licensed Arizona contractor."

## Poor fit
Renters without owner authority · cheapest-box price shoppers rejecting a
load calc · DIY installers / equipment-only buyers · off-grid or
winter-inaccessible cabins · commercial, 5+ multifamily, specialty
manufactured-home equipment (refer out) · envelope-failure homes (weatherize
first — say so).

## Qualified lead
In service area + owner/decision-maker + residential + install/replacement/
conversion job + ~90-day timeframe + reachable. Missing area/owner/residential
= disqualified; missing timeframe = nurture.
