# Audience / ICP — Najam Vozila

The single home for WHO. Persona, questions, objections, triggers, poor-fit.

## Who the customer is
Age 25–60; slight male skew for business/long-term rentals, roughly 50/50 for tourist rentals. Income €30K–€90K+. Segments:
- Tourists landing at Croatian airports needing a car for holiday or touring — Western/Central European (DE, AT, IT, UK, FR, NL, CZ), Scandinavian, North American.
- Business travelers and companies/sole traders needing a reliable vehicle short- or long-term.
- Domestic renters without their own vehicle or with a temporary need (breakdown, servicing, relocation, seasonal work).
- Expats and relocators living across Croatia (Zagreb, Split, Dubrovnik, Zadar, Rijeka, Pula, Šibenik, islands).
- Partners: hotels, agencies, insurers, assistance providers (B2B channel).

Rental occasions: vacation road trips (7–14 days), business travel (2–5 days), long-term personal/corporate use (1–12 months), replacement vehicle while own car is in repair or after an accident, weddings/events (premium vehicles), seasonal work.

## Who is not a fit
Drivers under 18 or without a valid licence (held under 1 year); people seeking a rental without a contract/insurance; buyers wanting purchase or lease-to-own; renters likely to break terms (driving in excluded zones, transporting animals, smoking in the vehicle); anyone without a credit card for the deposit.

## Top pre-purchase questions
What's included in the price? What's the mileage allowance? How much is the deposit and how is it charged? What's the minimum age? Do I need a credit card? Can I pick up/return at the airport or at an address? Can I cross the border or take a ferry? What happens if there's damage or an accident? Can I add a driver or child seat? Can I do a one-way rental?

## Objections
Fear of hidden fees; deposit size and card hold; unclear insurance coverage and excess (franšiza); vehicle availability in peak season; young/senior driver fees; cross-border restrictions; doubts about vehicle age/condition; "the aggregator price was cheaper"; "why not just use a taxi/Uber?"; "do I really need full insurance?"; "is manual the only option?"

## Demand triggers
Incoming flight + holiday; business trip; own car breakdown/service; traffic accident (replacement vehicle needed); relocation/move (van/cargo vehicle); weddings and events (premium vehicles); seasonal work; expiring current rental; rising cost of vehicle ownership/insurance (pushes long-term rental over ownership, especially for expats).

## Search intent types
Local ("rent a car Zagreb/Split/Dubrovnik," "najam auta [grad]"), airport/transactional, comparison (price/terms, "najbolji rent a car"), urgent (replacement vehicle, last-minute), business/enterprise and long-term, informational (terms, age, deposit, insurance, how to book). Multilingual search behavior: English, German, Croatian ("rent a car Croatia," "najam vozila," "Mietwagen Kroatien," "dugoročni najam vozila Zagreb"). Typically compares 3–5 providers, checks an aggregator (Rentalcars, DiscoverCars, Kayak) then the direct site for a better price.

## Content preferences
Road-trip itineraries with rental tips, insurance explainer guides, short- vs long-term cost breakdowns, ferry/island access guides, seasonal driving guides (winter tyres, summer A/C), border-crossing rules, fuel-type guides, Dalmatian-city parking tips, rental vs. public transport vs. private transfer comparisons.

## Conversion triggers
Transparent all-inclusive pricing, no-hidden-fees framing, free cancellation policy, real fleet photos, airport pickup confirmation, multilingual support (EN/DE/IT/HR), positive Google/Trustpilot-style reviews, clear insurance explanation, competitive long-term monthly rates, included/unlimited mileage, fast online booking with instant confirmation.

## Croatia-specific context relevant to this audience
Narrow, winding Dalmatian coastal roads; island access requires a ferry (some providers restrict island use — must be declared); tolls on A1/A3 motorways; summer parking in Split/Dubrovnik/Zadar extremely limited; winter driving in Lika/Gorski Kotar requires winter equipment Nov–Apr; cross-border travel to BiH/MNE/SLO/IT common but requires pre-authorization; fuel prices above EU average; growing local trend toward long-term rental over ownership due to rising vehicle/insurance costs.

## What qualifies a lead as sales-ready
Valid driver's licence (≥1 year) and credit card in hand; meets age requirements; has clear dates and location. For B2B: registered legal entity, defined need (fleet size, duration), realistic budget.
