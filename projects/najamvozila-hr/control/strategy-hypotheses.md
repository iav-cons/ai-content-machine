# Strategy Hypotheses — Najam Vozila

Owner strategy input to research: priorities, hub ideas, page wishlists, KPI
targets. These are hypotheses to VALIDATE — entity research and the cluster
must confirm, adjust, or reject each with a reason. Research determines
architecture; this file never predetermines it. The approved cluster and URL
map override anything here.

## Commercial priorities (validate against real margins/volume)
- Highest current volume/revenue: seasonal short-term rental (economy + SUV) at coastal airports.
- Best margin: long-term rental (30+ days) and premium/SUV categories.
- Strategic growth (not yet top revenue): long-term rental for companies/sole traders, B2B/partner program, off-season rental — aimed at reducing summer-only seasonality.
- Seasonal/campaign plays: early-bird booking, winter/off-season discounts, 30/90/180-day long-term packages, weekend packages, group/transfer offers.
- Entry-tier acquisition play: aggressively priced economy class in peak season to capture search volume and new customers (possible loss-leader).

## Hub/spoke hypotheses (validate, don't obey)
- Hypothesized hubs: "Ponuda vozila" (fleet/categories), "Lokacije/Aerodromi" (city + airport pages), "Dugoročni najam."
- Hypothesized spokes: individual vehicle categories/models, individual cities/airports, FAQ/terms, travel-and-driving blog content.
- Support pages: contact, privacy, impressum.
- Disambiguation requirement to validate: because "najam vozila" is a mixed-intent SERP term (short-term vs. long-term/leasing vs. chauffeured hire in Croatia), consider a dedicated "kratkoročni vs. dugoročni najam" contrast section/page, cross-linked to the long-term hub.
- Watch for cannibalization: city page vs. airport page for the same city; short-term vs. long-term pages for the same category; duplicate seasonal campaign pages.

## Suggested supporting topic pool (from entity research — validate before building)
Pricing per day/category/season; fleet by category; rental conditions (age, licence, documents, young/additional driver); deposit and credit card; insurance and excess (CDW/TP/SCDW); pick-up/return + airports + delivery + fuel policy; cross-border rules; islands/ferries; short-term vs. long-term contrast; booking/cancellation; extras (child seat, GPS, winter gear); driving-in-Croatia (tolls, winter equipment, fines); alternatives (car sharing, chauffeured hire, taxi); FAQ.

## KPI hypotheses (validate cadence/targets with owner)
- Growth in organic traffic on transactional terms (city/airport/category pages).
- Number of completed bookings and qualified long-term/B2B inquiries.
- Conversion rate on landing pages.
- Share of off-season and long-term/B2B rentals (target: reduce summer dependency).
- Average rental value.
- Reviews/rating volume and quality.
- Timeline hypothesis: 90 days — core pages + working booking flow live; 6–12 months — ranking on local/airport terms with a steady flow of qualified inquiries.

## Admission checks for any new page idea (validate-don't-obey)
1. Does it reinforce one of the core entities (rental itself, location, vehicle category, terms/insurance, long-term/B2B)?
2. Would the right buyer actually search for it (genuine rental intent or qualified pre-rental question)?
3. Does it map to a real step in the customer journey or a real offer?
4. Can the brand say something differentiated on it (all-inclusive price, unlimited mileage, delivery, 0–24 support, local knowledge) rather than generic information?
5. Does it strengthen the site as a clear hub, spoke, or support page without creating cannibalization or orphan pages?

## Expansion hypotheses
- Go deeper into core first: all locations/airports, all vehicle categories, long-term and B2B, FAQ/terms — before going broader into adjacent informational topics (driving/travel in Croatia).
- Acceptable adjacency: driving/travel guides, parking/roads/tolls, vehicle-category selection, seasonal tips — always tied back to rental.
- Automatic "no" regardless of search volume: vehicle purchase/sale, lease-to-own, driving schools, motorcycles/boats/campers (unless launched as a separate line), chauffeured/taxi transport, financial/credit products.
- Tangential topics (general tourism) → separate blog/section on the same site with a clear link back to rental, not a separate brand.
