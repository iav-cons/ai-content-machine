## Context

### Brand and business model
NajamVozila.com is a Croatian rent-a-car (najam vozila) business operating without a driver, positioned as a domestic Croatian brand (not a foreign chain) built around the highest-volume market term "najam vozila." Core promise: all-in pricing (insurance, VAT/PDV, unlimited mileage, roadside assistance) with no hidden fees, vehicle availability at major airports and cities, and airport/address delivery.

Scope exclusions (confirmed business boundaries): does not sell vehicles, does not offer lease-to-own/leasing, does not provide chauffeured transport, taxi, or ride-hailing, does not organize trips or accommodation, does not offer uninsured or contract-less ("grey market") rentals.

### Core offers and fleet
- Short-term rental (daily/weekly) — largest revenue volume, seasonal peak (May–September), concentrated at coastal airports (Split, Zadar, Dubrovnik, Pula, Rijeka).
- Long-term rental (30+ days, monthly basis) — strategic growth priority; higher margin, more predictable revenue.
- Airport rental and vehicle delivery.
- B2B/partner program (hotels, agencies, companies, assistance providers) — commission, B2B pricing, potential white-label.
- Fleet categories: city/economy, compact, estate (karavan), sedan/limousine, SUV, SUV 4x4, luxury, passenger van, cargo vehicle.
- Fleet vehicle brands stated in sources: Renault, Dacia, Škoda, Opel, Volkswagen, BMW, Mercedes-Benz.

### Geography and service footprint
Operates across Croatia. Pick-up/return available at major airports: Zagreb (Franjo Tuđman), Split, Dubrovnik, Zadar, Rijeka, Pula. Head office referenced in the Zagreb Airport / Velika Gorica area. Delivery available in cities and by arrangement nationwide.
Priority markets: Zagreb (year-round, business + airport) and Split (largest summer volume). Secondary/seasonal: Dubrovnik, Zadar, Pula, Rijeka. Tertiary: delivery to smaller towns and continental Croatia on request.

Operational figures referenced in sources (delivery fee ~€50/event beyond 40 km, out-of-hours pick-up/return fee ~€60/event, cross-border and young-driver surcharges, deposit amounts, etc.) are explicitly modeled on a reference operator (Alpha Drive Rent a Car) for illustration and are **not confirmed** as NajamVozila.com's actual policies — see Operations truth section below.

### Legal and regulatory classification (market truth)
Short-term vehicle rental without a driver is classified in Croatia as a tourism service under a special form of tourist offer per the Zakon o pružanju usluga u turizmu (NN 130/17, 25/19, 98/19, 42/20, 70/21), Article 95. Providers must have at least one business premises, publish general rental terms (Croatian and/or English), and notify/register with the Ministarstvo turizma i sporta (Središnji registar). This is legally distinct from long-term rental/operational leasing (12+ months, business-oriented) and from chauffeured vehicle hire (regulated under the Zakon o prijevozu u cestovnom prometu). General contract basis: Zakon o obveznim odnosima. Vehicle/driver conduct: Zakon o sigurnosti prometa na cestama. Winter equipment (M+S tyres/chains) is a legal requirement 1 Nov–30 Apr on designated road sections.

### Market entities and competitors
Central market entity: "Najam vozila" (short-term rent-a-car), commonly co-referenced as "rent a car," "najam automobila," "najam auta," and legally as "iznajmljivanje vozila." The Croatian SERP for "najam vozila" mixes three intents: short-term rental, long-term/operational rental, and chauffeured hire — disambiguation content is a market reality, not a brand claim.
Domestic competitors: Carwiz, ORYX, Last Minute Rent a Car, Nova, Avax, Optima, RentX, HAK Rent a Car, Avia Rent a Car, Autowill, Lutar, Loop, Euro Media (rent.hr), Connitor, Car-Rent.hr.
International competitors: Sixt, Hertz, Avis, Europcar, Budget, Enterprise.
Long-term/subscription-adjacent: Auto Hrvatska Mobility, Moove On (Grand Automotive), AutoZubak Lease.
Aggregators: DiscoverCars, Rentalcars.com, Skyscanner, Kayak.
Trust anchors in the market: Croatian airports, HAK (Hrvatski autoklub, roadside assistance), Zelena karta (green card) cross-border insurance system, Jadrolinija (ferry/island travel), Europski centar za potrošače Hrvatska (consumer rights).
